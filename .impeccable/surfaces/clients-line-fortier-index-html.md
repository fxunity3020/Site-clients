# Surface brief: Line Fortier Coiffure Elle & Lui (site client)

Scope: one-page site for the salon at 749, boulevard Cadieux, Beauharnois. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: Beauharnois locals, women and men, families and long-time clients, mostly on phones in daylight.
Action: call the salon (450 429-2222). The callback form is secondary.
Proof on hand: 30+ years on boulevard Cadieux, 5,0 Google (60 avis), 405 abonnés Facebook (all from the previous version, to reconfirm). Services and durations are examples to confirm. Hours unknown.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, no build, Cloudflare Pages; light, never dark (user); keep the 3D barber chair (user); no salon photos, video or old logo (user); fr-CA; reduced motion respected.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the salon's own hairstyle magazine from the waiting-room table. It refuses the category default: dark luxe salon or beige spa hero with a "Réserver" button over a row of service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Glossy cover and inside pages. Cover shot on a saturated copper-red seamless (the salon's red-haired poster), black leather chair as cover star, white extended-black masthead, black condensed coverlines, a yellow round sticker. Inside: cool glossy white paper, black ink, red folios and running heads, Archivo across widths only. Rules, columns, drop cap, pull quote, tear-out reply card with dotted cut line.

STORY: A neighbour sees a mixed salon that has done their street's hair for 30 years, Elle and Lui alike, believes they will be listened to, and calls.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed red cover. Masthead "Elle & Lui" across the top, "Line Fortier Coiffure" above it small. The 3D chair centred-right, rotating, at cover-star scale. Coverlines left as buttons (Coupes femme, Couleur, Coupes homme, Dégradés). Price box bottom-left replaced by the phone number as the primary call button. Sticker: "30 ans sur le boulevard Cadieux".

FORM: Hairstyle magazine, position 5 of 7 on my ordered list; seed:14c4a39d1dba4d82. Signature interaction: a coverline click turns the chair to its part and answers in the sticker; inside pages are Sommaire, the Elle/Lui spread, the portrait feature, Courrier des lecteurs (FAQ), the reply card (contact), back cover.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
